# Retroactive Rewards

The **Retroactive Rewards** phase represents our desire to give special recognition to groups of supporters who have had significant impact on our MASQ journey so far, and to showcase how users and community can engage with MASQ to help us grow (this links to our upcoming Community Initiatives).

These Retroactive awardees are rewarded granted $MASQ for helping in various ways, whether these members were in the trenches testing the Early Access MASQ browser constantly, helping report bugs and feedback or simply just being constantly engaged on socials to spread the word of MASQ’s mission to the wider web3 space.
